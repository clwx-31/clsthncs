Tracker

Log it, or it didn't happen

Progressive overload requires knowing what you did last time. This tracker stores everything in your browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded anywhere, there's no account, and nobody else can see it. Export a backup regularly, because clearing your browser data will erase it.

Back up your data. This lives only in this browser, on this device. Clearing site data, using a different browser, or private browsing will lose it. Hit Export JSON every few weeks and keep the file somewhere safe. A paper notebook is an entirely respectable alternative to all of this.
