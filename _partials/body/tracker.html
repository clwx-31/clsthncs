Log it, or it didn't happen

Progressive overload requires knowing what you did last time. This tracker stores everything in your browser's local storage — nothing is uploaded anywhere, there's no account, and nobody else can see it. Export a backup regularly, because clearing your browser data will erase it.

Back up your data. This lives only in this browser, on this device. Clearing site data, using a different browser, or private browsing will lose it. Hit Export JSON every few weeks and keep the file somewhere safe. A paper notebook is an entirely respectable alternative to all of this.

Progress photos

The site tells you to photograph yourself every four weeks because you are the person least able to notice your own change. This is where they live. They are stored in this browser on this device only — there is no upload, no server, and no account. Nobody but you can see them.

This browser doesn't support local image storage, so photo comparison is unavailable here. Everything else on this page still works. Keep your photos in a dated folder on your phone instead — the protocol for taking them matters far more than where they are kept.

Your data

Everything is stored locally in this browser: your weigh-ins and measurements, your workout log, your baseline tests, your program start date, the rung you're on for every ladder, and every checklist and milestone box you've ticked across the site. Export gives you all of it in one JSON file you can re-import here or on another device.

Importing only replaces the sections the file actually contains, so an older backup won't wipe newer data it doesn't know about.
