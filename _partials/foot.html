This site is educational, not medical advice. Bodyweight training is low-risk, but you are still loading joints and connective tissue. If you have a pre-existing injury, a heart or joint condition, or you get sharp pain that does not go away, talk to a doctor or a physical therapist before continuing.

Built for a 5'9", 150 lb beginner training three days a week, starting from the floor. All units are US customary (lb, in, oz, °F).
