Printable

Session cards & wall calendar

Paper versions of all nine sessions and a 24-week calendar to cross off. Useful when your phone is across the room, when you're training outside in the cold, or when you just want the tally on the wall where you can see it.

These use your saved rungs. If you've run the rung finder, the cards name the exact variation you should be doing and print the one cue that matters for it. Otherwise they show the beginner defaults. Rep ranges and rest come straight from the program, so a card and the site can never drift apart.

Printing tip: choose landscape for the calendar and portrait for the cards, and turn off headers and footers in the print dialog.

Baseline test sheet

The twelve tests from the baseline protocol, with four columns — week 1, week 9, week 12, week 24. Warm up 5 minutes first, rest 3 minutes between tests, one attempt each, full range only, and run them in this order every time. Write 0 where you can't do one; that zero is the number that will look most dramatic later.

Do not lose this sheet
